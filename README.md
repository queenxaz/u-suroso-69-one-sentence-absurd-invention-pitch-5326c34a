A single sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny
everyday annoyance, for the make.fun bounty
`u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a`.

## The entry

> The WelcomeMat 3000 loudly rates every visitor's shoes out of ten the
> moment they wipe their feet, so nobody tracks mud through your house
> ever again — they're too busy arguing with the floor.

The annoyance: guests tracking mud through the house. The invention: a
doormat that loudly scores every visitor's shoes out of ten the moment
they wipe their feet. The silly mechanism: nobody ever gets as far as
the hallway mud — they're too busy standing on the mat, arguing with
the floor about their score.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the one sentence above to `/output/entry.txt`
(1–220 characters, UTF-8). No network, no randomness, no dependencies.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
